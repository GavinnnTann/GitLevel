The quarter

Your level is a career.
Your rank is right now.

Levels are quadratic and honest, which means a veteran's card barely moves. So there's a second axis running underneath it: every calendar quarter is a season, and season XP counts only what you shipped inside it. One line on your card. It resets in January, April, July and October.

Mythril30,000

Nothing decays. Ever.

A quiet quarter shows a low rank — and touches nothing else. Your level, class, tier and badges are untouched by seasons, and no rank you've earned is ever taken back. Decay is how a tool that's meant to flatter you starts making you resent it.

No multipliers.

Tenure, combo and fame exist to make a career legible. A season is just “what did you make in these 90 days”, so it reads exactly the same for someone in their first month and someone in their fifteenth year.

Finished seasons go on the shelf.

Your character sheet keeps every quarter you've played. A season's mark can only be earned while that season is running — there is no way to go back and collect one later.
